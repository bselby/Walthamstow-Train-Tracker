About E17 Trains

Made in Walthamstow by a parent whose toddler loves trains. It tells you when the next train will pass your spot, so you can get to the railings in time.

Star the spots you use and they stay at the top. If you star just one, the app opens straight on it.

Where the times come from

Live times from the TfL Unified API, with freight from Realtime Trains. We add our best guess for how long each train takes to reach your spot.

Freight data contains information provided by Realtime Trains and is used here for non-commercial hobbyist purposes.

Help time the trains

Tap the yellow button as a train passes and you'll help make the times better for everyone.
